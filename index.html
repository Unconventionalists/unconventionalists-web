Why we exist

Most businesses are built around people doing repeatable work. We start from the other end.

What would a company look like if software did the work from day one, and people only stepped in for the decisions that matter? That is the question Unconventionalists is set up to answer, one venture at a time.

We use AI to scan markets for gaps that are too small, too niche or too fast-moving for a conventional team to chase. When a gap holds up, we build the product, the marketing, the customer service and the finance as one automated system, launch it, and let it run.

Each venture is a separate, revenue-generating business. Unconventionalists is the parent that finds them, builds them and keeps them honest.

What stays human

Minimal human-in-the-loop is not the same as none.

Automation does the work. A small number of decisions stay with people, every time, because they carry consequences a model should not own.

  • DirectionWhich markets we hunt in, and which we leave alone.
  • The go/no-goNo venture launches without a person reading the evidence and saying yes.
  • MoneySpending limits, pricing floors and where revenue goes are set by hand.
  • Law and ethicsCompliance, data handling and anything that touches someone's wellbeing.
  • The off switchEvery venture can be paused by one person in one step.
