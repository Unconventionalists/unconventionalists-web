01 Why

Most of what it takes to run a small company is now something software can do.

We use AI to find overlooked opportunities, then build and launch businesses that run with almost no one at the controls.

Finding a gap in a market. Writing the copy. Building the product. Answering the email at two in the morning. Each of these used to need a person who was good at it, awake, and paid. Now most of it can be done by agents, around the clock, for very little.

So we are running an experiment. Can a company be found, built, launched and run with almost no one in the room? Not as a stunt, but as an ordinary business that earns more than it costs.

People are still here. We decide what to pursue, what to stop, and what is acceptable. The companies are built so that the rest doesn't need us.

A note on the word experiment. Everything we try is written up in the log, whichever way it goes. A venture that fails is still a result.

02 How it works

Five stages. A person signs off on two of them.

  1. 1

    Scan

    Agents read markets, forums, filings and complaints, looking for problems people already pay to solve and nobody serves well.

    No human · runs weekly
  2. 2

    Decide

    A shortlist with evidence lands on a Friday. One idea goes forward, with a budget and a test.

    Human · we choose
  3. 3

    Build

    Agents write the product, the site, the copy and the plumbing: payments, support, accounts.

    Human · review before launch
  4. 4

    Launch

    Real page, real price, real traffic. The test is money, not interest.

    No human
  5. 5

    Run

    It runs. We read the log, set the limits, and decide when to stop or scale.

    Human · we keep the off switch

Every venture passes through the same five stages. What happened at each is written down, whichever way it went.
